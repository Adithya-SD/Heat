How does it work?

Is it private? Video, audio and chat go directly between the two devices. Switch on the end-to-end lock and they are also sealed with a key that never leaves the invite link, so even the relay servers cannot read them.

Do I need an account? No. Start a room, share the code, QR or link.

Stuck on “Connecting”? Tap “Check connection” on both devices; it names the cause.
