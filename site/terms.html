What Heat is

Heat is free software that connects devices for video calls and motion-camera viewing. It depends on third-party signaling, STUN and relay servers that can be slow, change or go away. It is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind, and without any promise of availability, quality or fitness for a particular purpose.

Using it responsibly

  • Only point a camera or microphone at people and places where you have the right to, and tell people they are being recorded or watched where the law requires it. Laws on recording and monitoring differ between countries and states.
  • Do not use Heat to harass, spy on, threaten or deceive anyone, to share unlawful content, or to break the law.
  • Do not rely on Heat for emergencies, medical or safety-critical monitoring. It can fail without warning.
  • Recording a call is your responsibility. Heat tells the other person when you start, but you must still have their consent where required.

Security

The end-to-end lock protects a room only as well as the key is kept secret: anyone you give the link, QR code or key to can join and read it. Keep rooms you care about locked, and compare the safety code if you do not use the lock.
