Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Heat is a video calling and motion-camera page that runs in your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. The short version: your video, audio and chat go from your device to the other device, not through a Heat server.

What stays on your device

  • Settings and the last room code you used (localStorage): your name, camera and microphone choices, video quality, glass effect level and similar.
  • If you join a locked room, the room key is kept in sessionStorage for that tab only, so “Reconnect” works. Closing the tab removes it.
  • Recordings you choose to make are saved to your own device and are never uploaded. When you record a call the other person is told.

What leaves your device

  • Video, audio and chat travel directly between the devices (WebRTC), encrypted in transit. With the end-to-end lock on they are also sealed with a key that only the invite link carries, so even the relay servers cannot read them. Without the lock, they are protected in transit but not against a hostile signaling server.
  • Signaling. To introduce two devices, the page sends the room code and connection details (including IP addresses) to a signaling server. By default this is the public PeerJS server at 0.peerjs.com, which is run by the PeerJS project, not by Heat. It never sees your video, audio or chat.
  • STUN and relay (TURN) servers. To find a path between devices the page contacts STUN servers (Google, Twilio) and, when a direct path is impossible, relay servers (PeerJS, Open Relay by Metered). They see your IP address and the encrypted traffic, nothing readable.
  • The website host (for example Vercel) receives the usual request data when you load the page: IP address, browser, time. Heat does not add anything to that.

Rooms and access

A room exists only while its host has the page open. Anyone with the code can ask to join; for video calls the host can be asked to approve each person. The room key, when the lock is on, is separate from the room code and is never sent to any server.

Camera and microphone

The browser asks before the page can use your camera or microphone, and you can revoke that at any time in the browser’s site settings. The motion camera analyses frames on the device itself.

Your choices

Clear the site’s data in your browser to remove everything stored on your device. Because nothing about you is stored on a Heat server, there is nothing for us to delete or export.

If someone else runs this copy of Heat

Whoever hosts a copy chooses its signaling and relay servers (see the CONFIG block in the page source) and is responsible for its hosting logs. This page describes the default build.

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/Adithya-SD/Heat. This is a plain-language description, not legal advice; if you run Heat as a public service, have it reviewed for your jurisdiction.
