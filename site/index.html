Heat

Your old phone, watching for you.

Heat turns a spare phone into a motion-detecting camera. Watch it live on your wrist or in a browser, and get an alert the moment something moves. No accounts, no cloud, free.

Get the apps

Android. The phone app is the camera; the watch app (Wear OS) is the remote and viewer.

Phone app · Android Watch app · Wear OS
How to install
  1. Open the downloaded Heat.apk on the phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from this browser once.
  2. Open Heat, allow the camera, and leave it where it can see the room.
  3. For the watch, turn on Wi-Fi debugging in the watch's developer options, then run adb install Heat-watch.apk from a computer. Opening the watch app starts the phone's camera by itself.
